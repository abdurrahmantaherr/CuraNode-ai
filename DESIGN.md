# DESIGN

Status: started in Phase 0, completed in Phase 5. Supersedes docs/DESIGN.md for visuals.

## Direction
Calm, premium, clinical but warm. Mint and teal atmosphere, frosted glass shell, soft
rounded forms, tall capsule shapes, circular gradient badges, a dark floating pill
navigation with a white active pill.

## Dials
VARIANCE 4, MOTION 3, DENSITY 4 (patient) and 6 (doctor).

## Rules carried forward
- One focal element per page. At most one primary button per view.
- Each fact appears once on a screen.
- Glass only on the sidebar, mobile pill nav, page hero, passport card, doctor next-up card.
- Never invent data; hide the element instead.
- No em-dashes or en-dashes in code, templates, copy or comments.
